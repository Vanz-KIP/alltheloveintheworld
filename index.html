Happy Birthday,
Dani.

On the day you were born, the world gained another life worth celebrating. So I hope, on your birthday, you remember to live that life too.

Not just to survive through the days, not just to become who you think you're supposed to be, and not to rush toward some version of yourself you haven't reached yet.

Take your time. Go places, try things, make stupid mistakes, laugh until your stomach hurts. Let yourself change your mind, let yourself become someone you haven't imagined yet.

There is no deadline for becoming, and there shouldn't be a deadline for living either.

So today, and in all the days that follow, memento vivere.

— Vanz
